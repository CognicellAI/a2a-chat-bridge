# Ubiquitous language

## Direct-message policy

An operator-declared allowlist of Agent aliases available in every private 1:1
bot conversation, regardless of adapter. It is distinct from platform
membership: the chat platform decides who can open a bot DM; this policy decides
which remote Agents that private Surface may invoke.

## Conversation policy

An operator-declared allowlist of Agent aliases for one shared Slack
conversation or all workspace threads below one Discord parent channel.

## Surface

One bridge-owned scope for a private DM, shared group DM, or shared thread.
Sessions and remote context IDs never cross Surface boundaries.
